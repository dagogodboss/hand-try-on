# Hand Try-On Counter

Browser AR try-on for watches, bracelets and nail colours. It tracks your hand with the webcam (MediaPipe Hand Landmarker) and renders 3D pieces on your wrist and fingertips (three.js). Everything runs in the page; no video leaves the device.

Live: https://dagogodboss.github.io/hand-try-on/

- Press **Start camera** and hold your hand and wrist in view.
- **Window mode** turns it into a hands-free shop display: swipe your hand left or right to browse.
- The catalogue and prices are invented sample data.

Single file, no build step: `index.html`.
